# Goblin Horde: setup, Explorer map, VFX and sound manifests

## Setup (people and agents)

1. Clone https://github.com/hotaq/game-roblox. Install Rojo 7.7 (`brew install rojo` on macOS), then run `rojo plugin install` once.
2. Open the place in Studio (place id 96482005947199).
3. From the repo root, run `rojo serve roblox/default.project.json`. In Studio, open Plugins → Rojo (the 7.7 one) → Connect.
4. Before connecting, make sure your files are up to date (`git pull`): the first sync writes the files over Studio.
5. Edit `roblox/**/*.lua`. Each save syncs into Studio in about a second. Never edit synced scripts in Studio itself.
6. Claim a Partyboard task (https://acp-roblox.vercel.app) that names the files or spans you will touch. Play-test, then commit, push, and post evidence on the task.

Only the scripts listed in `roblox/default.project.json` are synced. Anything else in the place, such as models, maps, animations or test scripts, is never touched by Rojo.

## VFX manifest

Textures are in `GoblinConfig.Textures`; the source PNGs come from `scripts/07_vfx_textures.py`.

| Key | Asset | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Slash | 125436767772213 | sword slash crescent (horde), sword trail (Humanoid goblins) |
| Spark | 73368593132738 | slash sparks, impact sparks, telegraph glint, stun stars |
| Burst | 101702985444399 | hit flash |
| Dust | 102159467271043 | footsteps, lunge, slam, body fall |
| Smoke | 78889473112923 | death smoke |
| Ring | 89996613812409 | heavy-attack telegraph, slam shockwave |
| Pixel | 119273301334400 | death dissolve cubes |

Horde effects, in GoblinHordeClient:
- Sword slash: a Beam crescent with 5 looks by sword level (`SLASH_TIERS`: wood, gold, double, energy, legendary).
- Fireballs, Fire Ring, XP gems, floating damage numbers.
- A white hit flash: a Highlight from a pool of 24, because Roblox renders at most 31 at once.
- Flinch on stunning hits.

Humanoid goblin effects (GoblinVFX) fire from the timing events in GoblinAnimData: trail, eye light, glint, stun stars, dust, sparks, smoke and the death dissolve.

## Sound manifest

The ids are in `ReplicatedStorage/Goblin/GoblinSounds.lua`. Every one is a free Creator Store audio that loaded in this experience. GoblinSFX plays them as 2D sounds for the local player. Each sound has a cooldown, so a 100-goblin wave can't stack copies.

| Sound | Asset | Plays when | Cooldown |
|---|---|---|---|
| Slash | 134671877618939 | your sword slashes (pitch rises a little per sword level) | 0.05 s |
| Fireball | 118217695193917 | your Fireball fires | 0.08 s |
| Hit | 139520673393967 | your hits land (HordeHits) | 0.06 s |
| GoblinPop | 72264591133889 | goblins die (HordeDied) | 0.05 s |
| GoblinSwing | 134671877618939 (pitched down) | a goblin starts its 0.55 s wind-up, so you know to step away | 0.6 s |
| Gem | 2575934454 | you pick up XP; pitch climbs during a pickup streak | 0.04 s |
| Hurt | 126202409049252 | your health drops | 0.3 s |
| LevelUp | 112485797063762 | a new level offers cards | 0.5 s |
| Reroll | 72264591133889 (pitched up) | cards are re-offered (reroll or a queued level) | 0.1 s |
| GameOver | 129285831966291 | the run ends | 1 s |

To swap a sound: find a free Audio on the Creator Store and check that it loads in Play (`Sound.IsLoaded`, `TimeLength > 0`). Then change the `Id` and note the `Source`. Never guess an id.
